Process

How a sale works

  1. 01

    Pick a ticket

    Filter the bench. Read the spec and condition. Price is on the amber sticker.

  2. 02

    Hit Hold

    Hold opens a mail to Dee with the SKU in the subject. Dee replies yes or gone. No cart, no card on this page.

  3. 03

    Pay + pickup

    Cash or the method Dee names. Pickup after confirmation. Sold tickets stay on the wall so the lot looks honest.

Rules

Pickup

City and exact address come from Dee after a hold is confirmed. Placeholder until Dee pastes the real neighborhood.

  • Holds last until Dee says otherwise — usually same day or next evening.
  • Test on site if you want. Once it leaves the bench it is yours.
  • No shipping on v1. Local pickup only.
  • Sold means sold. Do not email that SKU.
