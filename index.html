How a sale works

Three steps. No cart.

    Pick a ticket

    Open SKUs can be held. Sold stays on the wall so you know the pace.

  2. 02

    Email the hold

    Each card opens mail to dnccomputerz@gmail.com with the SKU in the subject.

  3. 03

    Meet after yes

    Dee confirms, you pay as agreed, you leave with the box. City TBD on refine.

  • — Local pickup. City and hours land in the refine pass.
  • — Cash / agreed transfer. No card reader on v1.
