Process

How a sale works

  1. 1

    Pick a ticket

    Filter the lot. Read the spec and condition. Price is the sticker.

  2. 2

    Hit Hold

    Hold opens mail to dnccomputerz@gmail.com with the SKU in the subject. Dee replies if it is still on the bench.

  3. 3

    Pay + pick up

    Cash or the method Dee names in the reply. Pickup after confirm. Shipping is Phase 2.

Rules

Pickup

City / hours

City and window get written in when Dee sends them. Until then: local pickup only, after a Hold is confirmed. Do not show up cold.

Holds + sold

A Hold is a conversation, not a cart. If the ticket says SOLD, it is gone. Dee flips that sticker in the HTML for v1.
